Executive Summary

What the public evidence shows

The findings point to a public that is highly dissatisfied with the status quo, more open to a federal settlement than simple rejection figures imply, supportive of renewed negotiation, and socially divided by the depth of intercommunal contact. Baseline hopes and concerns reveal the expectations citizens bring to the issue before exposure to any pro- or anti-settlement communication.

Integrated Research Report

Reading the findings together

Substance. Dissatisfaction with the status quo is overwhelming, while BBF occupies a broader zone of acceptance or toleration than a simple support/opposition binary would suggest. The coexistence of substantial tolerance for both BBF and a unitary state demonstrates that public preferences cannot be reduced to one mutually exclusive constitutional choice.

Baseline expectations. Before communication exposure, respondents already carry a mixed set of material, mobility and institutional expectations. Hopes around property, movement and youth employment coexist with concerns about refugee return, political dysfunction, daily tensions and cost. These baseline measurements are presented descriptively; the public report does not examine how communication subsequently altered them.

Process. A large majority prefer some active route back to negotiations. Restarting from Crans-Montana is the single largest specified route, while citizen assemblies also attract a meaningful constituency. A minority prefer not to restart negotiations.

Intercommunal relations. More than half report no contact with Turkish Cypriots, while only a minority report monthly or more frequent contact. Contact is positively associated with affective warmth, underlining the importance of the social-relational environment alongside constitutional preferences.

Interpretive limit. These findings describe and explain public opinion. They should not be read as a referendum campaign plan. Analyses whose primary value lies in identifying persuadable groups, effective messages or candidate optimisation are intentionally outside this public platform.
