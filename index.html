Executive Summary

What the July 2026 evidence shows

The survey depicts a society that overwhelmingly considers the present situation unfair, remains open to more than one constitutional alternative, prefers renewed diplomatic activity, and continues to experience limited everyday contact across the communal divide.

92.4%

judge the status quo unfair.

75.3%

accept or tolerate a bizonal, bicommunal federation (BBF).

84.9%

report at least some knowledge of what BBF is.

29.1%

answer “don’t know” when asked about the fairness of the Crans-Montana convergences.

82.8%

prefer an active route for renewed negotiations.

51.7%

report no contact with Turkish Cypriots.

Knowledge of BBF

54.4%say they know it quite or very well

Figure 2. Fairness of the Crans-Montana convergences

Fair/unfair percentages are calculated among respondents expressing a substantive fairness judgment. “Don’t know” is reported among respondents answering the item, so the percentages have different denominators.
